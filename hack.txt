DeFi has reached escape velocity, and it’s now being adopted all around the world. There are hundreds of protocols that offer different instruments and yield opportunities, and a few dozens are already battle tested. The world is about to understand how DeFi can be used to fund and grow ecosystems, and Octant is leading the charge. Octant v2 aggregates yield generating protocols and combines them with allocation mechanisms, making growth for any ecosystem sustainable and ever-lasting.

DeFi strategies and their integration is a crucial for Octant v2’s vaults. We’re looking for builders who can bring innovation, uniqueness, and an interesting perspective to DeFi strategies, design, and public goods for our vaults.

Learn more about how we use ERC-4626 for our protocol.

About Octant
Octant v2 is an ecosystem engine designed to efficiently transform idle capital into sustainable growth. It seamlessly connects various web3 revenue streams such as protocol fees or yield from DeFi strategies into customizable growth and incentive programs.

Octant will be a shelling point for yield-generating strategies used to power different projects within the broader Ethereum ecosystem. We’ve given over $12 Million in grants funding to impactful projects in Ethereum. Read more.
